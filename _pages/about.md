I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Alireza Vahid.

My research focuses on wireless localization, resilient positioning, navigation, and timing, low-Earth-orbit satellite navigation, non-terrestrial networks, and intelligent multi-source navigation systems.

My current work investigates passive LEO-assisted navigation using Starlink beacon signals, GNSS/LEO/IMU sensor fusion, and adaptive trust orchestration for resilient navigation under jamming, spoofing, and changing satellite availability.

Previously, I worked on RIS-assisted indoor localization at millimeter-wave frequencies and on energy-efficient optical network design.

## Research Interests

- LEO satellite-based navigation
- Resilient positioning, navigation, and timing
- Wireless localization and sensing
- Non-terrestrial networks
- GNSS/LEO/IMU sensor fusion
- Agentic AI for communication and navigation systems
- RIS-assisted mmWave localization
